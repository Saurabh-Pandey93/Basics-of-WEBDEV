About

Welcome to our Aviskari Nexus classroom for rural collages!
Here, everyone can learn, share ideas, and work together easily.
Let’s help each other and grow together.

Features

💡 Idea Sharing & Collaboration
Share your thoughts and work together with friends and teachers. Everyone’s ideas matter!
🧑‍💻 Live Coding Sessions
Learn coding by joining live classes. Ask questions and get help instantly.
🤝 Teamwork & Mentorship
Form groups, help each other, and get guidance from mentors for better learning.
📚 Study Materials & Resources
Access notes, books, and videos anytime to make your studies easier.
📝 Assignment Submission
Complete your homework and submit it online without any hassle.
💬 Discussion Forum
Ask questions, clear doubts, and talk with classmates and teachers.
